# Roadmap

## Current foundation

Repository identity, legal texts, architecture, governance, native documentation and dedicated validation tools are being qualified. Domain capabilities and claims remain empty.

## Capability milestones

Complete source and reference-role selection precedes actual prior-plan acquisition and seven independent circuit oracles. Literal-bit and whole-buffer admission precede finite error metrics and genuinely applicable bounds. Six statistical methods need their own full population, source and coverage qualification.

The four public operations then need real storage, receipts, installed evidence provider and independent consumer qualification. Complete sweep semantics preserve individually tested minima and all later failures. Native counterpart behaviour, documentation, benchmarks, recovery and applicable lifecycle evidence accompany each implemented capability.

Fabric support requires actual qualified capture and timebase. Public activation, release and external reproduction require their own evidence. No availability date or numerical or performance advantage is promised.
